Section 1 • Quick Start

Quick Start

Connect the keyboard, play a sound, and change it.

STEP 1
Connect: Connect the MPK Mini IV USB-C port to the USB host port of the ESP32-S3 with a USB cable. The ESP32-S3 supplies power to the keyboard. Connect headphones or powered speakers to the PCM5102A line output.
STEP 2
Power on: Supply 5 V to the ESP32-S3. The synthesizer loads slot 1 (JUNO-60). The MPK screen shows the preset name and 1/32 | BANK A.
STEP 3
Select a preset: Press < or > to go to the previous or next slot. You can also turn the push-turn encoder, holding SHIFT to move faster.
STEP 4
Change the sound: Turn knobs K1 to K8 to change the Bank A parameters. Press BANK A/B once to use the same knobs for the Bank B parameters (K9 to K16). Press it again to go back to Bank A. The MPK screen shows the parameter name and value.
STEP 5
Set the volume: Select Bank B and turn the eighth knob (K16 Master, MIDI CC 7). Every preset has Master on K16.
Note: Knob changes are not saved. When you select a different preset, its knobs go back to their default values and the knobs return to Bank A. To keep a sound, save it to a user slot (16 to 32) over Bluetooth LE before you change presets. See Section 3.

Section 3 • Connections & Setup

Connections, Patches and Troubleshooting

How the MPK Mini IV, the ESP32-S3 and the audio output connect, how to keep user patches, and what the status messages mean.

SIGNAL FLOW USB HOST • I2S • BLE

Keyboard Connection (USB)

  • Connect the MPK Mini IV directly to the ESP32-S3 USB host port (GPIO 19 D−, GPIO 20 D+).
  • The ESP32-S3 supplies 5 V to the keyboard. You do not need a separate power supply for the MPK Mini IV.
  • All performance MIDI (keys, pads, knobs, wheels, pedal) goes through this USB connection. The synthesizer does not use the MIDI OUT jack.

Audio Output

  • Connect headphones, powered speakers or a mixer to the 3.5 mm line output of the PCM5102A board.
  • The output is stereo at 44.1 kHz. Chorus and tremolo effects use the stereo field.
  • Set the volume with K16 Master (Bank B, eighth knob, MIDI CC 7).

User Patches (Slots 16 to 32)

  • Slots 16 to 32 hold user patches in the ESP32-S3 flash memory. They stay after power off.
  • Connect to the Bluetooth LE MIDI device MK4-Synth to send patches as SysEx.
  • To keep the sound you are playing, including your knob changes, send SysEx command 0x03 with the slot number (F0 7D 01 03 <slot> F7).
  • To send a complete patch from a computer, open tools/patch_sender.html in Chrome or Edge. It sends command 0x05 and can save to any user slot.
  • Command 0x04 with a slot number requests a patch dump (slot 0 = the sound you are playing). The synthesizer answers a save with an ACK or NACK.

Status Display (ESP32-S3 OLED)

  • The small OLED on the synthesizer shows the active preset, the last knob that you turned and an animation.
  • The top-right corner shows the output mode: STEREO or MONO. While the metronome runs it also shows the tempo and time signature (120 4/4 ST), with a bar under them that flashes on every beat (thicker on beat 1). Set the mode with Rhythm page K6.
  • Warnings show as a short tag in the header and a banner for 3 seconds. See the table below.
